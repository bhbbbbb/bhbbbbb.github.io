# Purchase Prediction via Automatic Tagging


## Two stage algorithm

1. Tagging for every product
1. Simple algorithm that make prediction using tags of session products
    - Readout
    - Similarity function

## Why NOT End-to-end Machine/Deep Learning

- Hard to collaborate
    - The process is not separable
- Hard to implement/debug
    - Data scientists are poor in programming
    - Training takes time
- Hard to innovate

## Pros & Cons

- Pros
    - Low latency
    - Highly interpretable
    - Much easier to innovate
- Cons
    - ML/DL may preform better
        - We can claim the prediction performance is just one of the assessments.
        - Think about the limit of ML/DL...
    - This is a ML/DL course
        - Generative AI
        - Some simple ML (e.g. xgboost) methods can be incorporated, serving as baseline
